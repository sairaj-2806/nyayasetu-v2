## 3. Verification & Compliance Summary

- **Section 63 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA, 2023)**: Admissible electronic evidence certificates generated with SHA-256 hash preservation, device serial numbers, and cryptographic chain-of-custody verification.
- **Section 105 & 193 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS, 2023)**: Mandatory electronic records of search/seizure panchnamas and charge sheet filings securely archived with version tracking.
- **Zero-Trust Security**: Validated against OWASP Top 10 API Security, IDOR vulnerabilities, path traversals, prompt injections, and privilege escalations.
